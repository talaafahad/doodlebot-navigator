    How to play

    1. Build a maze: pick a tool and click or drag on the grid. Place Doodlebot and Princess-Bot.
    2. Explore: press Start Exploring. Doodlebot can't see the maze! It senses the cells inside its circle and draws its own map.
    3. Rescue: when its map is finished, Doodlebot goes back to the start and finds the shortest path to Princess-Bot.
    4. Experiment: change Mission Settings → How far can Doodlebot sense? and run the same maze again. Compare in My Experiments.

    Click Doodlebot any time to ask it a question, by typing or with your voice.
